## Phases 1–15: complete

These phases established drawing, calibration, plants, layers, constraints, exports,
climate calendars, seed inventory, companion planting, crop rotation, garden records,
CAD precision, smart features, Agent Integration, 3D visualization, and the visual refresh.
See the canonical roadmap for the one-line delivery index and the ADR/FR references.
