# 3D Look & Ground Pitfalls (PF-L3D)

| ID | Rule | Status | Where | Legacy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PF-L3D-01 | A gate that the repair itself makes true proves nothing, so measure before the repair, make the repair report what it changed, and never make a constraint hold by translating the result. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-01) | 11.4.12 |
| PF-L3D-02 | A plant in a raised bed casts from the bed's soil and is lifted only while its link holds and its stable geometric centre, not its `boundingRect()` centre, lies inside the bed's shape. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-02) | 11.4.13 |
| PF-L3D-03 | A bound that keeps the topmost N records drops what everything stands on, so a tile loses its smallest records by how much of the tile their paint covers, not by count. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-03) | 11.4.17 |
| PF-L3D-04 | A `Texture` element holds one texture for life, because destroying any texture it once held nulls its `textureData` and turns the tile white, so a re-baked tile is a new Model with a new texture. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-04) | 11.4.17 |
| PF-L3D-05 | Hand the engine every colour derived in linear light through `engine_srgb`, build each sky as a new `Texture` with its own data, and stop the wind clock whenever the pane is hidden. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-05) | 11.4.18 |
| PF-L3D-06 | A blended custom material must keep the frame's alpha (`sourceAlphaBlend: Zero`, `destinationAlphaBlend: One`): with the colour factors on alpha too, a translucent overlay brightens the view by `a(1 − a)` of the window's white instead of darkening it. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-06) |  |
| PF-L3D-07 | An unlit overlay passes through the mood's exposure, so a 3D shadow drape multiplies the frame and an overlay colour is divided by the exposure. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-07) |  |
| PF-L3D-08 | A hidden or blended Model drops out of the shadow map, so a part that must keep casting while it fades or is cut is an opaque screen door (a fragment discard) that collapses in the AO depth pass. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-08) |  |
| PF-L3D-09 | The 3D drape follows the shared Hours of Sun both ways, also when 3D opens with it on. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-09) |  |
| PF-L3D-10 | Render production plants and verify final seasonal bounds. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-10) |  |
| PF-L3D-11 | A `CustomMaterial`'s `TextureInput` ignores the `Texture`'s `flipV`, so a shader that samples a north-up ground tile flips `v` itself. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-11) |  |
| PF-L3D-12 | Do not switch on scene-wide order-independent transparency: `OITWeightedBlended` re-blends L2's multiply drapes and brightens what a shadow drape covers. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-12) |  |
| PF-L3D-13 | Never fill round the plan with a large surface just under it: a triangle kilometres wide is centimetres off in depth and wins against the plan. Keep one plane, cut the plan out, cells of at most 100 m. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-13) |  |
| PF-L3D-14 | Do not feed changing geometry through QML's `ProceduralMesh`: replaced lists kept drawing the first data, and one created later drew nothing. Use a `ReloadableGeometry` on a declared Model. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-14) |  |
| PF-L3D-15 | Keep `aoStrength` 0 wherever ambient occlusion is off: Qt's `setAoStrength` switches AO on whenever the strength is not 0, whatever `aoEnabled` says. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-15) |  |
| PF-L3D-16 | Derive held context from both records' consumers and admit lawn blades only where the active preset draws them; detail geometry must degrade to exact prisms below its dimensional floor. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-16) |  |
| PF-L3D-17 | A solid that should keep its 2D fill shows it on its TOP only, through a per-item `baseColorMap` (`MeshPart.fill_texture`, `vcPart.patternMap`) built from the canvas's own tinted texture — one picture per (pattern, fill colour), never one per item — with UVs `(position + paint_origin) / 256` (the canvas's phase, its `params["paint_origin"]`). Its SIDES are the item's tint (`walls_mesh`, no UVs): one fill-textured prism smears the picture down the edge. A raised flat item that also has relief (a lawn's blades) keeps it, lifted to the new top, and the height gates (`top_cm`/`fit_scale`) ignore `ground_detail` parts. A raised pond's water takes the same picture (`fill_texture` on a `water` part; `water.frag` samples `uFill`). Every `Texture` gets its data at birth: a `Texture` re-pointed at another `QQuick3DTextureData` goes white. Bind a `var`-typed map as `patternMap ? patternMap : null`; `baseColorMap: patternMap` with a null value logs "Unable to assign [undefined] to QQuick3DTexture*" and the render gate fails on it. A fill part's fade tint (`tintLinear`) is its fill colour even when the picture's `tint` is white: a `vc` part's screen door samples no picture (a faded lawn is its colour), while the water door samples `uFill` through the same branch, so a faded raised pool keeps its caustics. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-17) |  |
| PF-L3D-18 | Measure an idle 3D view only after its first build was PRESENTED (a frame after the request, then a quiet stretch), and measure a frame cost A/B interleaved round by round — never in blocks. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-18) |  |
| PF-L3D-19 | A probe that compares "near" and "away" pixels of a perspective frame must bin them by the world quantity it cares about (elevation, bearing, distance) — an image row is a plane, not a cone. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-19) |  |
| PF-L3D-20 | A probe marker sized in world units has no guaranteed screen size: before a pixel gate judges it, check that the whole marker is clear and size it from the camera, then pin the measured pixels. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-20) |  |
| PF-L3D-21 | A structure's 2D picture and its 3D build read ONE parametric layout; never two tables of constants, and never a fixed sprite for one of them. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-21) |  |
| PF-L3D-24 | A shadow map fitted to the casters (`lockShadowmapTexels: false`) is sharp at a low sun but follows the camera: fit it only for a frame whose camera holds still — a photo, an agent's picture and a held camera's time-lapse ask `Quick3DHost.capture(fitted_shadows=True)` by name — and keep the texel-locked sphere for the live view, an orbit, a flight and any capture that does not ask; locked, a cascade spans the sphere of its whole slice up to `shadowMapFar`, and `pcfFactor` is a radius in scene units that cannot hide a texel larger than it. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-24) |  |
| PF-L3D-25 | Before blaming a renderer for a pattern in a shadow, check that it changes when the shadow map does: a "jitter" made from a multiplicative step modulo a small number is a RAMP, and a low sun stretches a ramp in a caster's height into a regular staircase along its shadow. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-25) |  |
| PF-L3D-26 | Under a weather whose sky is the light, ambient occlusion takes the weather's LIGHT share of a stronger, wider pass AND a bias of at least 0.3 of its reach (`weather.AO_STRENGTH_UNDER_CLOUD` 99/55, `AO_DISTANCE_UNDER_CLOUD` 70/14, `AO_BIAS_UNDER_CLOUD_CM` 21): Qt's pass reads a camera-facing normal, so flat ground seen at a slant occludes itself everywhere except beside a nearer object — a grey ground with a light rim round every silhouette. Never raise strength or reach without the bias; keep the bias a share of the reach at every picture size (`aoBias: root.aoBias * root.captureAoDistanceScale`). Clear keeps the spike's 55 / 14 / 0. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-26) |  |
| PF-L3D-27 | Clamp every array bound a probe computes from a camera before slicing with it: a horizon row above the frame is NEGATIVE, and `mask[:row]` with a negative `row` takes every row but the last ones — the "sky" mask of a downward camera covered 82 % of the frame and the probe judged only its bottom sixth. | standing | [11](../11-3d-look-ground-build.md#pf-l3d-27) |  |
