# 3D selection

- **FR-3D-SELECT-01** (L1.8): There is one selection, and the plan and the 3D view show the same one. An item selected on the plan is marked in 3D by a thin ring in the plan's selection colour round its footprint — round every outline of it, the holes of a shape included — where it stands (a plant on a raised bed: at the bed's soil). The ring has the same width on screen from every distance, is visible from every side and through whatever stands in front of it, and follows the item when it is moved. In 3D-only mode the selection stays: the ring and the info card show it, so Delete or Cut act on what is shown.
- **FR-3D-SELECT-02** (L1.8): A click in the 3D view selects what a click on the plan would: a click on an item selects it alone; Ctrl+click or Shift+click adds it to the selection or takes it out; a click on the ground or the sky clears the selection. A drag — moving the camera — never selects. The properties panel and the status bar follow as after a click on the plan, and a selection is not an undo step.
- **FR-3D-SELECT-03** (L1.8): Layers are respected: an item on a locked layer cannot be selected from 3D — the click goes through it to what lies behind, as it does on the plan (with nothing selectable behind it, a plain or Shift click clears the selection and a Ctrl click keeps it, as on the plan) — and an item on a hidden layer is not shown and cannot be hit. A click on a member of a group selects the group, and every member is ringed.
- **FR-3D-SELECT-04** (L1.8): Selecting never changes the 3D view itself: the picture does not move, jump or change its size when something is selected or the selection is cleared.
- **FR-3D-SELECT-05** (L1.8): While exactly one item is selected, an info card below the 3D view says what 3D shows of it: its name and type; for a plant its common and scientific name, its height and canopy diameter on the plan date — the numbers its 3D model was built from — and the bed it stands on; for a built item its height and its size, diameter, length or area. A plant the plan gives no height says "not known". An item that has no 3D model says why. Lengths are shown in the units of the rest of the application. "Show in Plan" centres the plan on the item (from 3D mode it switches to Split). With no item or several selected the card's place shows a hint instead. Text from a plan file is always shown as plain text.
