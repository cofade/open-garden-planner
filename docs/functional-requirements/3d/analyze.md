# Analyze in 3D

One "Analyze" menu in the 3D pane's strip (#386, ADR-063).

- **FR-3D-ANALYZE-01** (L2.1): The 3D ground can show the plan's own 2D sun analysis — **"2D Shadows on the Ground"** lays the shadows the plan's sun study draws on the canvas (the same shapes, cut at the plan's edge as the canvas cuts them, tinted like its shadow colour; the whole plan at night), following every move of the sun at once — they only ever DARKEN what they cover, in every light from noon to night; **"Hours of Sun on the Ground"** lays the hours-of-sun map the canvas shows (the same cells in the canvas's colour ramp, laid over the lit ground, south at the south edge) and switches the shared Hours of Sun on, so the toolbar shows it on too; the 3D ground follows the shared Hours of Sun both ways — switched on in the toolbar, the map replaces what lay on the 3D ground (a 3D view opened while it is on shows it at once), and switched off (in the toolbar, or by opening another plan), what lay on the 3D ground before comes back (the 2D shadows, or nothing). **"Nothing on the Ground"** removes it. The 3D view's own shadows stay as they are: illustrative; the drape is the analysis. What stands on the ground hides the drape under it.
- **FR-3D-ANALYZE-02** (L2.1): With the hours of sun on the ground, a click on the ground (not on an object) reads the map where the click meets the ground: the place below the 3D view says how many hours of direct sun that spot gets on the map's date and its band (full sun, partial sun, partial shade, full shade — the glossary's thresholds), and a pin marks the spot. Without a computed map, or outside it, it says so; while the plan has changed since the map was computed, it says the hours of sun are being updated and reads the spot again when the new map is ready (or says that it could not be computed). Choosing another drape removes the pin. The click takes the one picture a click always takes and changes nothing in the plan or its history.
- **FR-3D-ANALYZE-03** (L2.2): **"Sun Path and Compass"** draws where the sun goes on the plan date, on 21 June, on 21 December and on 20 March of the plan's year — four arcs on a dome over the garden, from the plan's location and the sun's own position (no chosen angle), with a bead on every whole hour of the plan date and the sun itself where it stands at the toolbar's time; the arcs keep the legend's colours in every light — and a compass rose on the ground around the garden with the four cardinal letters in the user's language and a north arrow. A legend below the strip names the arcs — and, when the plan date's arc lies on one of them (its solar noon within half a degree, within days of a solstice), says so ("21 June — same path as the plan date"); without a garden location it says that one is needed for the arcs.
- **FR-3D-ANALYZE-04** (L2.2): **"Sweep the Day"** plays the plan date from sunrise to sunset (ten minutes of sun every tenth of a second), moving the shared time — the toolbar, the 2D shadows and the 3D light follow — and the hours-of-sun map on the 3D ground fills up as the day passes; at sunset it is the whole map and the time goes back to where it was — also when it is stopped, when the date changes or the 3D view closes; a time the user sets during the sweep ends it and stays. Without a location, or on a day without sun, it says so and does not start. A sweep takes no picture of the 3D view.
- **FR-3D-ANALYZE-05** (L2.3): For a plant, the info card also says where its height comes from (grown to the plan date, as measured, the species' maximum, set on the object, estimated …), how far along its growth it is (only for a plant that grows with the plan date), the hours of direct sun the Hours of Sun run measured for it with their band, and its windows for sowing indoors, sowing outdoors and harvest in the plan's year (from the plan's last spring frost, the same dates the task list uses). A built object's height also names its source.
- **FR-3D-ANALYZE-06** (L2.3): **"+1 Year"**, **"+3 Years"** and **"+10 Years"** move the shared plan date forward (29 February moves to 1 March in a year without one; the date range of the app bounds it) and then show a before/after of the 3D view in its place: the garden before the jump on the left of a divider the user drags (or moves with the arrow keys), after it on the right, each side named by its date; "Back to 3D View" or Escape closes it. "Back to <date>" returns to the date before the first jump. The comparison takes exactly two pictures of the 3D view; a jump never edits the plan.
- **FR-3D-ANALYZE-07** (L2.4): **"Cutaway"** takes the roofs off every building (a building added while it is on has none either; the inside of its walls shows) and, while something is selected — a bed or a path drawn on the ground included — lets every object that stands between the eye and the selection fade to a fine screen of itself, so the selection is seen; a faded object or a removed roof cannot be clicked (the click reaches what is behind it). Shadows stay as they are: a removed roof and a faded object still shade the ground. It follows the camera once it comes to rest, the selection and every edit; turning it off restores everything. Nothing in the plan changes.
- **FR-3D-ANALYZE-08** (L2): While the 3D pane is hidden the analysis does no work; when it is shown again it shows everything at once. A new or opened plan drops the probe, a sweep in progress and the way back of a growth jump, and keeps the user's other choices in the menu (the hours of sun follow the shared switch, FR-3D-ANALYZE-01). The strip's controls move onto a second line in a pane too narrow for one, so every control and notice is shown whole in every language.
