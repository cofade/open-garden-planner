## 1. Truth before beauty (non-negotiable)

OGP is a planning tool. A beautiful render that misstates the plan is a **P0**, full stop.

| Gate | Rule | How it is measured |
|---|---|---|
| Height | known height: mesh top = resolved height (`effective_height_cm(at_date)`) ±3 %; unknown plant height: top = the separately labelled illustrative category estimate (#557), never a recorded dimension | bounding box; `fit_to` enforces it for every plant archetype; estimate table = `core/plant_height_estimate.py`, analytical height stays unknown |
| Spread | widest crown span = 2D canopy diameter ±10 % | bounding box vs `plant_canopy_radius_cm` (`ui/canvas/footprints.py`; a record's `params["radius_cm"]`) |
| Base | a plant that is linked to AND stands inside a raised bed / container stands on its soil, not inside the box | base = `ui/canvas/footprints.item_base_cm`: a bed's effective height − 2 cm (the assumed soil drop: the plan holds no fill for a bed); a container's effective height itself (it IS its soil fill height — no lip is invented); 0 when the plant is not linked, or its centre is outside the parent's shape — its stable geometric centre (`footprints.standing_point`), never the centre of its paint-dependent `boundingRect()`: a warning badge must not move a plant off its soil. Creating a bed around an existing plant links and lifts it (#443). Remaining nesting limits, pinned by tests: a climber on a trellis in a raised bed stays at 0, a pot on a raised bed stands on the ground (ADR-037 addendum "base stacking") |
| Sun | light direction comes only from `core/solar` (never a "nice-looking" angle) | light = −sun vector (ADR-037 pin) |
| Shadows | engine shadow footprint of a box caster vs the analytic 2D shadow | IoU ≥ 0.85 at 15°/35°/60° (spike at 960×540: 0.98/0.98/0.96 on OpenGL **and** on Direct3D 11; 0.96/0.95/0.92 at 1280×720; orthographic, no cascades — table in `ogp-3d-renderer` §4). **Defined on a solid box caster.** Built-world meshes are not boxes: judge them against their own measured IoU with the 2D caster model at 15°/35°/60° (`scripts/measure_built_world_shadows.py`; `tests/unit/test_scene3d_shadow_agreement.py` holds the builders to it within 0.05), not against this gate — raised bed, box, plain panel 0.99–1.00 (the same prism); hedges 0.93–0.97; round tub 0.89–0.91 and terracotta pot 0.81–0.83 (taper); house 0.74–0.88, shed 0.83–0.88 (gable roof vs flat-topped prism); wall 0.96/0.91/0.81, stone-wall fence 0.94/0.87/0.73, hedge fence 0.96/0.89/0.76 (nominal section — the plan holds no thickness for a line: the 3D shadow is WIDER, more at a high sun); picket fence 0.65/0.66/0.68, metal fence 0.19/0.20/0.24, chain-link fence 0.12/0.13/0.19 (porosity: the 3D shadow is a FRACTION of the solid strip the 2D sun study casts — an open owner question, not a builder defect). L4 (#388, ADR-065): hip roof 0.79/0.74/0.83 (its tip 270 cm short with the sun square to the house, as the gable's), single-slope roof 0.94/0.93/0.91, flat roof 0.98/0.97/0.96; the objects are porous like the post fences — table 0.38/0.54/0.70, picnic table 0.37/0.41/0.57, grill 0.28/0.30/0.36, greenhouse 0.30/0.23/0.25 (its glass casts nothing), trampoline 0.47/0.64/0.76 — the same open owner question. **What the listed numbers are:** analytic references for the BUILDERS — one item, the sun square to a straight 10 m line (a building: square to or along its ridge), the mesh projected onto the ground without a shadow map. Against that reference a builder's number below the listed one is a finding; the listed number is not. They are NOT what an engine render must read: the engine's shadow map is judged on solid casters (the box gate above), and thin members — 0.8 cm wires, 1.6 cm bars, about one shadow-map texel — are exempt from a per-pixel IoU; a render that reads lower there is no builder defect. **Where the tip is** (reach along the shadow direction, 3D − 2D, cm at 15°/35°/60°, same script and test, within 1 cm): it agrees within 2.5 cm for the prisms (−0.6/−0.6/−0.3; plain panel 0.0), the pots (−0.6 … −0.3) and the post fences (picket fence +2.0/+2.0/+2.4, metal +0.8, chain link +1.5); hedges +2.7 … +4.4 (leaf tips). It does NOT agree for a gabled building with the sun square to its ridge — house −270.0/−270.0/−108.2, shed −150.0/−81.4/−21.0: the 2D model extrudes the footprint to the ridge height, so its top edge is at the far wall while the gable's ridge is mid-house, half the depth short at a low sun. That is the two models' difference, not a roof to "fix". Sun along the ridge: +30.0 (the overhang). Nominal sections reach further: wall +15.0/+15.0/+15.8, stone-wall fence +14.5/+14.5/+15.3, hedge fence +19.6/+19.5/+19.6. No universal sentence about shadows goes into this row unless the script prints the number behind it |
| North | ground texture is north-up; nothing is mirrored | orientation probe NCC: identity must win |
| Sky | the sky's sun disc sits at the solar azimuth | sky probe error < 6° (spike, disc 25° off-centre: max 0.85° OpenGL, 1.4° D3D11) |
| Date | season/growth shown = the plan's sim date | models are built for each shot's own date; every shot row records `sun_date` = `build_date` (`metrics.json`) |
| Season | fruit and flowers only inside the plan's OWN frost-free season: `location["frost_dates"]` `last_spring_frost` ≤ day ≤ `first_fall_frost` (`"MM-DD"`, the keys the task generator reads); fruit whose species carries harvest weeks follows the shared task generator's harvest window instead — last spring frost + `harvest_start` … + `harvest_end` weeks, anchored on every frost year whose window can reach the day (a 29 February frost and the spike's fallback for it: ADR-048 entry 18; `task_generator.generate_for_date_window`, behind the agent's `get_tasks` and `get_task_calendar`; every GUI task surface anchors on one year, TD-037; bench: tomato 18 Jun–27 Aug, sweet pepper 2 Jul–13 Aug, so the 21 June board shows no peppers); no frost dates → no seasonal claim (accents stay). Crowns never go bare: the growth model has no leaf-off — an owner question, not a look lever | `runner.in_frost_free_season`, `runner.in_harvest_window` / `accents_in_season`; every shot row's build records `in_season` (`metrics.json` `builds`); exact accent-colour markers in `test_spike_q3d_board.py` / `test_spike_q3d_meshes.py` |
| Built heights | every built object's SOLID top = its resolved height ±1 %; relief above it is not the height | builders exact to `h`, `fit_height` as the safety net; G1 (`verify_builder`) measures only non-`ground_detail` parts, so a raised lawn's blades may stand 0.3-9.4 cm over `h` (swept h = 1..200; 20.3-29.4 cm for h = 20) - every registered kind's solid is exact (44 kinds × h = 1/7/20/40/200). Every object kind has a default height (`DEFAULT_HEIGHTS_CM`; since L4's second owner pass also the parasol, fire pit, planter pot, cold frame, rain barrel, water tap, swing, hammock and pergola) and casts in 2D and 3D; only a plant without species data is built at an illustrative estimate (#557) |
| Faces | the sun lights the face that faces it | stored normal · winding normal ≥ 0.99 on every flat face (the first board lit the wrong roof slope) |

Never tune a domain number (species height, spread, planting date) to make a shot prettier.
