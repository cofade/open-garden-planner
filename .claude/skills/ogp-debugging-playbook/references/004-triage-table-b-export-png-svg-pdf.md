## Triage table B — export (PNG / SVG / PDF)

| # | Symptom | Likely cause | Discriminating experiment | Fix pointer | Incident |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Export image completely empty (right size, background colour only) | Negative-height target rect: `QRectF(0, H, W, -H).isEmpty()` is `True` in PyQt6 → `scene.render()` clips to nothing | `print(target_rect.isEmpty())` before `scene.render()` | Painter pre-flip instead: `translate(0, H_px); scale(1, -1)` with a positive rect; `H_px` = image height in **pixels** | Chronicle: `negative-height target rect`; debug-verbose case study |
| 9 | SVG export: a texture floods the drawing, bars or strokes run past their shape, a pattern has the wrong angle or scale (PNG fine) | `QSvgGenerator` drops painter clips, a brush's own transform and a pixmap's source rectangle. The export therefore records the scene below the painter and replays it (`src/open_garden_planner/services/svg_export.py`, PF-CAN-53); a flood means something reached the generator without going through the recorder | Open the SVG in a **real browser** — `QSvgRenderer` ignores `clip-path`. Compare with the PNG export on the same grid: `tests/integration/test_svg_export_fidelity.py` does exactly that | `record_scene` + `write_svg`; never paint the scene into a `QSvgGenerator` directly, and never repair the written file by pattern (the removed `_fix_svg_qt_texture_clipping` did, and missed the parametric roof of #768) | Chronicle: `does not serialize painter clip regions`; debug-verbose case study 219 |
| 10 | SVG texture tiles look upside-down / abstract mush | Not the Y-flip: a pattern in the user space of a flipped group is oriented like the PNG (measured in Edge and `QSvgRenderer`, #787). Look for a dropped brush transform instead (row 9) | Compare the roof's two halves with the PNG export | The recorder moves a brush transform into the world transform; the old `_fix_svg_pattern_yflip()` was removed | Chronicle: `SVG pattern tiles` |
| 11 | PDF: everything crammed in the top-left corner | `QPdfWriter` defaults to ~1200 DPI; layout math assumes points | Print `painter.viewport()` — device-pixel dimensions ≫ page points | `writer.setResolution(72)` **before** `setPageLayout()` (already done twice in `src/open_garden_planner/services/pdf_report_service.py`) | Chronicle: `setResolution(72)` |
| 12 | PDF: scene appears as a thin strip / offset despite correct-looking flip math | `QPdfWriter`'s painter starts with a **non-identity** transform (margins) | `print(painter.worldTransform())` before your first draw | Render scene to a temp `QImage` first, then `painter.drawImage(content_rect, img)` — never `scene.render()` directly onto the PDF painter | Chronicle: `directly on QPdfWriter painter`; debug-verbose case study |
| 13 | Exported image: labels gigantic or microscopic, or minimap thumbnail shows misplaced handles | `ItemIgnoresTransformations` items render at natural pixel size under `scene.render()` (no view involved) | Compare a 10 pt label's size in-app vs in export | `ExportService._prepare_text_for_export()` before render + `_restore_text_after_export()` after — it retargets ONLY constant-pixel-size text (`scene_rendering.draws_at_constant_pixel_size`); thumbnails hide such items first (§8.9.5) | Chronicle: `render at fixed device-pixel size` |
| 13a | Exported image: a text NOTE is the wrong size relative to the plan | Something forced a point size on a `TextItem` / callout text, which is sized in plan units and must scale with the plan (#789) | `tests/integration/test_export_text_notes.py` measures the note's ink in every export | Leave non-`ItemIgnoresTransformations` text alone; PF-CAN-08 | — |
| 13b | Print or PDF page: shadows or a roof painted on the margin, over the footer or the title block | A `paint()` called `setClipPath`/`setClipRect` without `Qt.ClipOperation.IntersectClip` and replaced the tile's clip (PF-CAN-54) | `tests/integration/test_pdf_report_pages.py::test_no_scene_painter_replaces_the_clip_it_was_handed` names the line | Pass `IntersectClip` | — |
